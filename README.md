TEXTBOOK STOCK ANALYSIS 

1. Executive Summary & Business Problem
This project analyses the availability of textbooks and shortages across classes and subjects to support purchasing and distribution decisions.The workbook shows that 192 of the 261 required copies are available, leaving 69 missing copies and an overall availability rate of 73.6%. Creative Arts and Computing have the largest subject-level shortages, with 22 missing copies each, while KG2 has the largest class-level shortage, with 29 missing copies.

2. Dataset Description
   
Source

The dataset is contained in "textbook_stock_analysis.xlsx", which includes textbook inventory records, summary tables, and charts.

Dataset Overview

- Total textbook records: 30
- Copies needed: 261
- Copies available: 192
- Copies missing: 69
- Overall availability rate: 73.6%

Data Dictionary

Class: Class associated with the textbook requirement
Subject:  Subject area for the textbook
Copies Needed: Total number of copies required
Copies Available: Number of copies currently available
Copies Missing: Difference between the number of copies needed and the number available
Availability Rate: Percentage of required copies currently available

Calculated Fields

Copies Missing: Copies Needed − Copies Available
Availability Rate: (Copies Available ÷ Copies Needed) × 100

Availability rate is not applicable when zero copies are needed.

5. Key Insights & Findings
- Overall textbook availability is 73.6%.
- A total of 69 copies are missing from the 261 copies required.
- Creative Arts and Computing have the largest subject-level shortages, with 22 missing copies each.
- KG2 has the largest class-level shortage, with 29 missing copies.
- Copies Missing indicates the size of a shortage, while Availability Rate shows the proportion of requirements that have been met.

6. Recommendations

- Prioritise purchasing for subjects with the largest shortages.
- Allocate available copies according to class requirements.
- Conduct regular inventory reviews to identify shortages early.
- Use the analysis to support textbook purchasing and distribution decisions.

8. Limitations

- The analysis reflects the inventory data available at the time of assessment.
- The results describe textbook availability and shortages but do not forecast future demand.
- Availability rates are not meaningful for records where zero copies are required.
